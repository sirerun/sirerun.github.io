Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

This policy describes how Sire Run, Inc. handles information through sire.run and the is-optimized.com website audit and report-delivery service. Other products may have their own policies.

Information we collect

When you use the audit service, we collect the website domain you submit, publicly accessible website content needed for the audit, and the resulting findings. If you request a report by text, we collect your mobile number, any name you provide, your request message, delivery records, and your consent choices. We also receive technical information such as IP address, browser information, timestamps, and error logs needed to operate and protect the service.

How we use information

We use this information to generate and share reports, send requested report links, respond to support requests, record consent and opt-outs, prevent abuse, and maintain the service. Where you separately agree, Sire Run, Inc. may contact you about AI services through the channels described in the disclosure presented to you. Marketing consent is not required to read the free website audit report or purchase anything.

Public audit reports

Website audit reports and their links are shareable. They may contain the scanned domain and public website findings. Phone numbers, consent records, private messages, and internal delivery records are excluded from public reports. Do not submit private or confidential website content.

SMS information and service providers

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We use service providers to host the service, store records, and deliver messages, including Twilio for SMS and Google Cloud for the audit service. These providers receive information needed to perform those services. We may also disclose information when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights and security.

Messaging choices

You can view the full HTML audit report without providing a mobile number or agreeing to marketing. If you choose text delivery, you initiate a message requesting the report. Reply STOP to a text to opt out. Reply HELP for assistance, or email privacy@sire.run. Message and data rates may apply. See our SMS Terms.

Storage and retention

We retain information as needed to provide the service, manage consent and opt-outs, resolve disputes, and meet applicable obligations. Report links may expire. We may retain limited consent and suppression records after a report expires so that we can honor contact preferences and maintain evidence of requests.

Your requests

Contact privacy@sire.run to request access, correction, or deletion of your information, or to ask about contact preferences. We may need to verify your identity. Some records may need to be retained for legal, security, or opt-out purposes. We will handle applicable privacy requests as required by law.

Site preferences and external links

The corporate site stores a theme preference in your browser when you select a display theme. Hosting and communications providers may process technical request information. Links to other websites are governed by those websites’ policies.

Security and changes

We use safeguards designed to protect information, but no service can guarantee absolute security. We may update this policy and will identify the updated date on this page. Questions can be sent to Sire Run, Inc. at privacy@sire.run.
